# Stage 6 final holdout protocol

The forecast targets support **if voting occurred at its information cutoff**. A certified result from weeks or months later is a proxy containing subsequent opinion and turnout changes. The 2020, 2022, and 2024 results and poll archives have all been inspected during model development. None is an untouched final holdout for the current specification.

The next usable near-election holdout is the last source snapshot available **before Election Day on 3 November 2026**. Some early voting will already have occurred, so record how much of the electorate may have voted before the cutoff when interpreting its target. Freeze the code revision, Stage 2 database hash, ballot registry, pollster ratings, model parameters, information cutoff, random seed, and the 75,000-draw forecast before retrieving any 2026 result. Preserve the frozen artifacts separately from subsequent refreshes. The [published version 0.20 forecast and hash manifest](../artifacts/calibration/frozen_2026-09-27_v020/manifest.json) are frozen as a long-lead diagnostic; it cannot be scored as a direct held-today label against November results. Use `python pipeline/freeze_stage6_holdout.py --output-dir artifacts/calibration/frozen_<cutoff-date>` for the final pre-election artifact.

After certification, score candidate winner log loss and Brier score, candidate-share absolute error and 80%/95% interval coverage, and the observed House, Senate, and governor seat counts against their frozen joint draw distributions. Show the Senate result under each published independent-caucus scenario rather than assign a caucus after seeing the outcome. Break race scores out by office, poll availability, and unusual ballot structure. Report exact race exclusions and the time between the information cutoff and election.

The 2026 election supplies one chamber-level outcome, so a single seat-count percentile cannot establish nominal 80% or 95% calibration. Compare its errors with the historical proxy replays, and record any model change as a new version after the holdout is scored. For a direct held-today check before Election Day, the [withheld future-poll audit](../artifacts/calibration/stage6_senate_future_polls.json) tests whether uncertainty covers later independent poll questions, with poll measurement error included; it does not reveal the latent vote share exactly.

The [machine-readable prelaunch gate](../artifacts/calibration/stage6_public_gate.json) records completed reviews of the governor approval feature and the 2020 House interval miss. The 2026 final holdout is a **postrelease evaluation**. Its result is not required to publish the model. The held-today counterfactual cannot be observed directly, so public reporting must explain what the historical and 2026 outcome comparisons do and do not establish.
